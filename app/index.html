    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10

    PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours, with one playthrough enough, no online trophies and no difficulty trophies. The three steps below are built from PowerPyx's roadmap and the official trophy descriptions.

    1. Step 1 — Finish the story (5 chapters, ~60–80 hours)

      Play the five chapters in order. All 12 story trophies unlock automatically and none can be missed. Do the optional missions as they appear, and scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you travel — progress counts toward the 100% checklist even before it becomes visible.

      ⚠ Before Chapter 1 ends, meditate at the first meditation spot at home — it is the one possibly missable trophy (Positive Mental Attitude). There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save: cheats disable trophies and saving.

      Trophies: the 12 story trophies, plus early progress on stunts, vehicles, activities and challenges.

    2. Step 2 — The 100% Completion checklist (~100 hours)

      After the credits, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion. You need 70 of the 80 challenges (the 10 hardest can be skipped), 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 50 of 66 weapons, and 300 stunts. Strangers & Freaks strands are not needed for 100%.

      Trophies: A Long Road (gold), Cunning Stunts, One Last Thing, Whip It Good, You WAiNKer!.

    3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup (~10 hours)

      Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the five side contacts' final missions (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the collectible card sets, garages and Scooter Bros registrations, and any miscellaneous trophies left (bank vaults, armored truck, gang operations, the 500 Criminal Profile actions). The platinum pops with the last trophy.

      Trophies: Strangers & Freaks (silver), the five contact trophies, Don't Touch My Deck, Little Boxes, On the Spot, Maximum Withdrawal, and the rest.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list is flagged as possibly missable. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

    ⚠ Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida

    There are 18 meditation spots in 10 locations, for Jason and Lucia. The first spot is offered at home in Chapter 1 and becomes permanently unavailable after that chapter. The game has no mission replay, so if you leave Chapter 1 without meditating there, the trophy is locked for that playthrough. Meditate at the home spot before Chapter 1 ends, and keep a manual save from the start of Chapter 1 as backup. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of the 18 spots — this trophy needs all 18.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; spot count: GTA Zone.

    General warnings (not missables, but run-enders)

    • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission (PowerPyx, Output Lag).
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving for that session (PowerPyx). Never use a cheat on your trophy save.
    • No online trophies. All 55 are single-player and offline (PowerPyx). No trophy needs multiplayer or DLC.
    • No difficulty trophies, no mission medals. One playthrough on the single difficulty is enough (PowerPyx).

    Everything else in the list is obtainable in free-roam after the story, per PowerPyx.
